Introduction

Welcome to Ctrl+Alt. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (ctrl-alt.app) and use our mobile applications. Please read this privacy policy carefully.

Information Collection and Use

Ctrl+Alt is designed with privacy in mind. We do not track you across sites or build profiles based on your usage behavior. Any local app data (such as preferences or bookmarks) is stored on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Vercel Hosting and Telemetry

Our website is hosted on Vercel. As part of their standard hosting operations, Vercel may collect standard telemetry data from visitors. This data may include, but is not limited to:

  • End-user IP addresses
  • Approximate geographic location
  • System configurations (such as browser type and operating system)

This information is used by Vercel for security, analytics, and performance optimization purposes. For more detailed information on how Vercel handles this data, please review the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

Except for our hosting provider as mentioned above, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for their commercial purposes.
