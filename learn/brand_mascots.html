Ad Psychology

What is Brand Mascots?

Brands use cute or human-like mascots because our brains are wired to trust faces. This tricks people into extending social trust to a corporate entity that just wants to sell them products.

Where it comes from

Classic Advertising to Digital NPCs

How it hooks you

Exploits anthropomorphism: human-like traits trigger the warmth and trust we normally reserve for people.

What the research shows

Studies show giving brands human-like traits measurably increases perceived warmth, trust, and positive evaluations.

Source: Aggarwal & McGill, 'Is That Car Smiling at Me?', Journal of Consumer Research (2007)

How to resist it

When a mascot recommends a purchase, imagine the corporate logo making the same recommendation.
