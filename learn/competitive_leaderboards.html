Social Pressure

What is Competitive Leaderboards?

Public rankings turn private goals like exercise or learning into visible competitions, where your position relative to others becomes the point.

Where it comes from

Fitness & Social Apps (2010s)

How it hooks you

Exploits the goal-gradient effect: being just below the next rank makes one more session feel necessary.

What the research shows

Studies find leaderboards increase activity and app-checking, while a meaningful minority of users report stress, guilt, or burnout from constant ranking.

Source: Hamari, Koivisto & Sarsa, 'Does Gamification Work?', HICSS (2014)

How to resist it

Switch to private or friends-only tracking, and measure progress against your own history instead of a ranking.
