Streaming Traps

What is The Rabbit Hole?

The 'Up Next' sidebar is designed to pull you away from what you originally searched for, often by recommending content that is more emotionally intense than what you are watching.

Where it comes from

YouTube Autoplay Era (2015)

How it hooks you

Exploits the curiosity gap: each recommended thumbnail promises something slightly more provocative than the current video.

What the research shows

In Mozilla's crowdsourced study, 71% of videos volunteers regretted watching were recommended to them by YouTube's algorithm.

Source: Mozilla Foundation, 'YouTube Regrets' report (2021)

How to resist it

Hide the recommendation sidebar with an extension like Unhook, and watch only what you came for.
