AI Deception

What is Data-Hungry Confidants?

AI companions invite your secrets: feelings, health, relationships. Those confessions become training data and marketing profiles held by whoever runs the bot.

Where it comes from

AI companion apps (2020s)

How it hooks you

Exploits reciprocal self-disclosure: intimacy cues trigger the human habit of opening up, but only one side of the conversation can be sold.

What the research shows

Mozilla reviewed 11 romantic AI chatbots and flagged every single one for privacy; 90% may share or sell your personal data.

Source: Mozilla Foundation, '*Privacy Not Included' review of romantic AI chatbots (2024)

How to resist it

Before disclosing something personal to a bot, ask whether you would post it publicly under your name. The chat window feels private; the data pipeline is not.
