Gamification

What is Loot Boxes?

Loot boxes sell a randomized chance at a digital reward for real money. Several countries classify the mechanic as gambling, and it is frequently aimed at young players.

Where it comes from

Online Gaming (2000s)

How it hooks you

Exploits variable rewards with real money at stake: paying for an unpredictable payout is the core mechanic of gambling.

What the research shows

Belgium declared paid loot boxes illegal gambling in 2018, forcing games to remove them for Belgian players.

Source: Belgium Gaming Commission ruling (2018); UK House of Lords gambling report (2020)

How to resist it

Block loot-box purchases with parental controls, and read odds disclosures the way you would read gambling odds.
