Deceptive Design

What is Forced Continuity?

Free trials convert into paid subscriptions automatically, often with minimal reminders and a cancellation flow that takes real effort to find.

Where it comes from

Subscription Services (2010s)

How it hooks you

Exploits inertia: easy signup plus difficult cancellation means procrastination alone extends the billing.

What the research shows

Hard-to-cancel subscriptions drew enough complaints that the US FTC moved to require cancellation to be as easy as enrollment.

Source: FTC Negative Option Rule proceedings (2023–2025)

How to resist it

Use virtual cards with spending limits for trials, and set a calendar reminder for the end date when you sign up.
