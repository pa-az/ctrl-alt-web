Social Pressure

What is Parasocial Relationships?

Regularly watching a creator builds a sense of friendship that flows only one way: you know them, they do not know you. Platforms and advertisers use that trust to sell.

Where it comes from

Horton & Wohl (1956), Influencer Era

How it hooks you

Exploits one-sided bonding: the brain treats familiar faces as friends, and recommendations from friends receive less scrutiny.

How to resist it

Keep in mind that an influencer's content is a performance, and your attention and trust are the business model.
