What is Phantom Vibration?

Frequent phone checking trains the nervous system to expect notifications, to the point where many people feel their phone vibrate when it did not.

Where it comes from

Mobile Phone Era (2000s)

How it hooks you

Exploits sensory expectation: a brain primed for unpredictable social rewards becomes hypervigilant and starts producing false alarms.

What the research shows

Up to 89% of undergraduates report experiencing phantom vibrations, on average about once every two weeks.

Source: Drouin et al., Computers in Human Behavior (2012)

How to resist it

Turn off vibration for set periods and carry the phone outside your pocket to weaken the conditioning.
