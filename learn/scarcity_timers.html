Ad Psychology

What is Scarcity Timers?

Countdown clocks and low-stock warnings push you to buy before thinking it through. The deadline is usually artificial; the pressure it creates is real.

Where it comes from

Retail Flash Sales to E-Commerce

How it hooks you

Exploits fear of missing out: a deadline reframes the purchase as avoiding a loss rather than making a choice.

What the research shows

Behavioral studies find scarcity cues increase urgency and willingness to pay, especially in impulsive buyers.

Source: Worchel, Lee & Adewole, cookie-jar scarcity experiment, JPSP (1975)

How to resist it

Avoid buying under a countdown. Save the item, wait 24 hours, then decide.
