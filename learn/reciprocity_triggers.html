Social Pressure

What is Reciprocity Triggers?

Follow-back prompts and notifications create a sense of social debt: someone followed you, so now it feels impolite not to follow them back.

Where it comes from

Social Networking Dynamics (2000s)

How it hooks you

Exploits reciprocity norms: unsolicited gestures create a feeling of obligation, and obligation drives engagement.

How to resist it

Treat follows as bookmarks, not favors. Follow only accounts you actually want to see.
