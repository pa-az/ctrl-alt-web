Intent Hijacking

What is The Home Feed Detour?

You open the app to do one thing. It opens onto a recommendation feed built to replace your plan with its own, and the thing you came for sits a search box away.

Where it comes from

Recommendation-first app design (2010s)

How it hooks you

Exploits intention interference: a vivid, novel stimulus presented before you act on a plan can quietly overwrite the plan.

What the research shows

71% of videos users regretted watching came from YouTube's recommendations, and recommended videos were 40% more likely to be regretted than searched ones.

Source: Mozilla Foundation, 'YouTube Regrets' crowdsourced study (2021)

How to resist it

Enter apps through search, subscriptions, or a direct link. If the home feed loads first, treat it as a detour, not a destination.
